Sugar Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Sugar Dash is an Android app that shows the current glucose, IOB and COB from AAPS on the Android Auto screen. It is made by Jan Votava.

What data the app uses

  • Data from AAPS on your phone: glucose values, trend, IOB, COB, predictions and range limits. AAPS sends them to the app on the phone.
  • Data from your Nightscout (optional): if you enter your Nightscout address and a read-only token, the app downloads glucose values of the last 2 hours to fill gaps in the graph. It uses https only.

Where the data is kept

All data stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. The app keeps only the last 3 hours of glucose and deletes older values. Your Nightscout address and token are also kept only on your phone.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send your data to the developer or to any other server. The only network connection is to your own Nightscout, if you set it up.
  • It has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • It does not share or sell any data.
  • It never sends anything to AAPS and cannot change any therapy settings.

Medical notice

Sugar Dash is not a medical device. Do not make treatment decisions based only on this app. Always check your CGM or AAPS.

Deleting data

Uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. This deletes everything the app has stored.
